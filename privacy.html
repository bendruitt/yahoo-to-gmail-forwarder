Privacy policy

This policy describes the private Yahoo to Gmail Forwarder utility, operated solely for its owner's email accounts.

Access and purpose: When enabled, the utility reads new incoming Yahoo messages and copies their original content and attachments into the owner's Gmail account. Its Gmail permissions are gmail.insert and gmail.readonly. Gmail read access supports verifying the destination account and reconciling uncertain imports to avoid duplicate copies.

Storage: Copied messages remain in Gmail, and originals remain in Yahoo. The owner's server stores protected authorisation credentials and a persistent processing checkpoint. The checkpoint records processing identifiers and status needed for retries and duplicate handling. Operational logs are designed to omit message bodies, attachments, passwords and tokens.

Sharing and use: Email content is transferred from Yahoo to Google at the owner's direction. The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. It does not provide mailbox access to other users. Any use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention: The owner can stop the utility, revoke its Google authorisation and Yahoo app password, and remove its server credentials and checkpoint. Stopping or revoking the utility does not delete original Yahoo messages or copies already stored in Gmail; those remain under the owner's mailbox controls. Checkpoints are retained while needed for operation and recovery.

Security: Credentials are held in protected server files and the owner's password manager, as appropriate. The service is configured without an inbound public service port. These measures do not guarantee against every security incident or data loss.

Contact: use the project discussion at project issues. Do not post emails, attachments, passwords or tokens. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page. Policy version: 2 November 2026.

Website hosting: These public information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. The pages add no analytics scripts, forms or cookies. GitHub may collect visitor information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. GitHub does not receive mailbox messages through these pages. Using GitHub Issues to contact the owner creates a public post.
