Terms of use

Yahoo to Gmail Forwarder is a private utility for its owner's authorised Yahoo and Gmail accounts. It is not offered as a service to other users, and no subscription or public account is available.

The utility is intended to copy new incoming mail while preserving Yahoo originals. Messages that cannot be safely processed may be held for review. Delivery can be delayed or interrupted by provider availability, expired authorisation, server failures or processing limits. The owner should retain access to the original Yahoo mailbox and check its status when delivery is interrupted.

Use is subject to the relevant Yahoo and Google account and API terms. The owner may stop operation and revoke authorisation at any time. No promise of continuous delivery or recovery of every message is made.
