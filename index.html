Yahoo to Gmail Forwarder

Yahoo to Gmail Forwarder is a private, single-owner utility designed to copy new incoming Yahoo mail into its owner's Gmail account. It preserves the original message, sender information and attachments. It leaves the Yahoo originals in place and does not send emails on behalf of the original sender.

The utility runs on the owner's self-hosted server. There is no public sign-up, hosted mailbox service or access for other users. Google authorisation is limited to adding messages and reading Gmail messages for account verification and duplicate reconciliation. It does not request permission to send, delete or change existing Gmail messages.

Contact: use the project discussion at project issues. Do not post emails, attachments, passwords or tokens.
